Is my financial data secure?

Your security is our top priority. All financial data is protected with bank-level encryption, secure API connections, and advanced authentication protocols. We never store your banking credentials, and your information is always kept private.

2

How do I connect my bank accounts or cards?

It’s easy — simply click “Connect Account” from your dashboard, choose your bank or card provider, and securely sign in. OneFin supports thousands of banks and financial institutions worldwide through trusted API integrations.

3

Does it cost anything to use the app?

OneFin offers a free plan with essential financial tracking features to help you get started. For advanced analytics, automation tools, and premium insights, you can upgrade to one of our flexible paid plans anytime.

4

What makes this app different from other finance tools?

OneFin combines smart automation, real-time financial insights, and a clean user experience in one platform. Instead of just tracking expenses, it helps you understand spending patterns, manage budgets smarter, and make better financial decisions faster.
